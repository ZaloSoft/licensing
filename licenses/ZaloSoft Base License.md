# Proprietary Source Code Access and Contribution Agreement

Copyright © 2026 ZaloSoft™. All rights reserved.

This Proprietary Source Code Access and Contribution Agreement (“Agreement”) governs access to, use of, and contributions to the source code, repositories, development systems, documentation, credentials, configuration files, build artifacts, deployment materials, internal tools, technical designs, and related materials of ZaloSoft™ (“Company”).

By accessing any Company source code, repository, development environment, internal system, documentation, artifact, or related material, the individual or entity accessing such material (“Contributor”) agrees to comply with this Agreement.

## 1. Proprietary and Confidential Materials

All Company source code, object code, scripts, configuration files, infrastructure definitions, schemas, documentation, product plans, internal communications, designs, credentials, secrets, test data, build outputs, deployment artifacts, and related technical or business materials are proprietary and confidential.

No ownership, license, right, title, or interest is granted to Contributor except the limited, revocable permission expressly approved by Company for authorized work purposes.

All rights not expressly granted are reserved by Company.

## 2. Authorized Access Only

Contributor may access Company materials only if:

1. Contributor has been expressly authorized by Company;
2. the access is necessary for Contributor’s assigned work;
3. the access occurs through Company-approved systems, accounts, devices, repositories, or environments; and
4. the access complies with all Company policies, agreements, security requirements, and instructions.

Access to one repository, file, project, system, branch, workspace, environment, or internal communication channel does not imply access to any other repository, file, project, system, branch, workspace, environment, or channel.

Contributor must not attempt to bypass, weaken, evade, or interfere with access controls, logging, monitoring, repository permissions, branch protections, code review rules, deployment controls, authentication systems, or security mechanisms.

## 3. No Unauthorized Use

Contributor may use Company materials only for legitimate Company work assigned or approved by Company.

Contributor must not use, run, execute, inspect, analyze, copy, modify, compile, test, benchmark, train on, derive from, or otherwise interact with Company materials for any purpose unrelated to authorized Company work.

Prohibited uses include, without limitation:

1. personal projects;
2. competing products or services;
3. side projects;
4. academic, research, portfolio, demonstration, or publication purposes;
5. training, fine-tuning, evaluating, or benchmarking any model, system, tool, dataset, or product not expressly approved by Company;
6. reverse engineering Company systems for non-work purposes;
7. sharing examples, snippets, patterns, architecture, prompts, schemas, infrastructure designs, or implementation details outside authorized Company work;
8. reusing Company materials in any non-Company codebase, product, service, repository, publication, or presentation.

## 4. No Unauthorized Copying or Local Storage

Contributor must not create, retain, export, download, clone, mirror, fork, cache, print, screenshot, photograph, transcribe, upload, or otherwise copy Company materials except as expressly authorized by Company and strictly necessary for assigned work.

Local copies are prohibited unless Company gives explicit approval or the copy is technically required by an approved development workflow.

Any approved local copy must be:

1. stored only on Company-approved devices, accounts, or environments;
2. encrypted where technically feasible;
3. protected by strong authentication;
4. kept only for the minimum period necessary;
5. excluded from unauthorized backup, sync, indexing, AI-assistant ingestion, telemetry, public paste services, and personal cloud storage;
6. deleted promptly when no longer necessary or when access is revoked.

Contributor must not store Company materials in personal repositories, personal cloud drives, personal email accounts, unmanaged devices, external drives, pastebins, note-taking applications, messaging apps, screenshots folders, browser downloads folders, or any unapproved system.

## 5. No Unauthorized Sharing

Contributor must not disclose, transmit, display, publish, distribute, forward, sublicense, transfer, or make available Company materials to any person, team, entity, system, tool, or service unless expressly authorized by Company.

This restriction applies to:

1. persons or entities outside Company;
2. external contractors, consultants, vendors, advisors, investors, customers, partners, auditors, or service providers;
3. internal teams, subsidiaries, affiliates, departments, or personnel who do not have explicit read permission;
4. public or private AI tools, code assistants, chatbots, code review services, logging services, analytics tools, debugging tools, or third-party platforms not approved by Company;
5. any repository, package registry, container registry, storage bucket, issue tracker, documentation system, or communication channel not approved by Company.

Contributor must obtain explicit Company approval before sharing Company materials with any outside contributor, external entity, external team, vendor, contractor, or internal person who does not already have authorized read access.

## 6. Outside Contributors

Outside contributors, including contractors, consultants, vendors, advisors, and non-employee collaborators, must:

1. keep all Company materials strictly confidential;
2. use Company materials only for the specific work approved by Company;
3. access only the repositories, files, systems, and environments explicitly approved by Company;
4. obtain explicit approval before adding personnel, subcontractors, agents, tools, services, or external systems to the work;
5. obtain explicit approval before disclosing Company materials to any third party;
6. return or delete Company materials upon request, termination, expiration, or completion of the engagement;
7. certify deletion, return, and disclosure of retained artifacts if requested by Company.

No outside contributor may subcontract, delegate, or share work involving Company materials without prior written approval from Company.

## 7. Security Obligations

Contributor is responsible for protecting Company materials against unauthorized access, disclosure, loss, theft, misuse, corruption, or compromise.

Contributor must follow security best practices, including:

1. using strong, unique passwords;
2. enabling multi-factor authentication where available;
3. protecting devices with encryption, screen locks, and current security updates;
4. keeping credentials, tokens, SSH keys, API keys, certificates, and secrets confidential;
5. avoiding hardcoded secrets;
6. avoiding public Wi-Fi or untrusted networks unless protected by approved security controls;
7. preventing unauthorized persons from viewing or accessing Company materials;
8. not disabling security tools, audit logs, endpoint protection, or access controls;
9. not using unapproved AI tools, extensions, browser plugins, IDE plugins, package registries, build services, or cloud services with Company materials;
10. immediately revoking, rotating, or reporting exposed credentials when required.

Contributor must not introduce malware, spyware, backdoors, logic bombs, credential harvesters, unauthorized telemetry, unauthorized dependencies, intentionally vulnerable code, or hidden functionality into Company systems.

## 8. Incident Reporting

Contributor must immediately report to Company any actual or suspected:

1. code leak;
2. unauthorized access;
3. unauthorized disclosure;
4. lost or stolen device;
5. compromised account;
6. exposed secret, key, token, certificate, password, database URL, environment variable, or credential;
7. vulnerability;
8. security breach;
9. accidental upload to an unauthorized system;
10. unauthorized use of Company materials;
11. violation of this Agreement.

Contributor must cooperate with Company’s investigation, mitigation, remediation, credential rotation, artifact deletion, forensic review, and disclosure obligations.

Contributor must not conceal, delay reporting, alter evidence, delete relevant logs, or attempt unauthorized remediation without Company approval.

“Artifacts” include any copies, clones, forks, patches, diffs, branches, pull requests, commits, notes, screenshots, logs, transcripts, terminal output, generated files, compiled binaries, containers, packages, database dumps, backups, test fixtures, credentials, environment files, model outputs, documentation, diagrams, and other materials derived from or containing Company materials.

Company may revoke Contributor’s access at any time, with or without cause.

Upon termination, expiration, suspension, reassignment, completion of work, or access revocation, Contributor must immediately:

1. stop accessing and using Company materials;
2. return or delete all Company materials and artifacts;
3. stop using Company accounts, credentials, repositories, systems, and environments;
4. disclose all retained artifacts, copies, credentials, and access methods;
5. cooperate with offboarding, audit, credential rotation, and verification procedures;
6. certify compliance if requested by Company.

Contributor’s confidentiality, ownership, assignment, security, deletion, non-use, and non-disclosure obligations survive termination.
